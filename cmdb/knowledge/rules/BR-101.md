id: BR-101
type: business-rule
title: Server hostnames are unique
status: draft
since: 2026-07-31
affects: [cmdb]
implemented_by: [cmdb]
verified_by: [CT-101]
---

A hostname identifies at most one server. Adding a server whose hostname is
already registered is rejected with HTTP 400 and
`{"success": false, "error": "Server already exists"}`.

Comparison is exact and case-sensitive (`WEB01` and `web01` are two
servers), because it rides on SQLite's default BINARY collation rather than
any explicit choice. Whether case-sensitivity is wanted is unknown; treat
that nuance as accident until confirmed.

Confidence: high for the rule. Enforced by `hostname TEXT UNIQUE NOT NULL`
(app.py:36) and the IntegrityError-to-400 mapping (app.py:451-452).
Confidence: low that case-sensitivity is intentional.
