title: Mutating a missing resource returns 404 with the success envelope

---

PUT and DELETE on a server, application, or service id that does not exist
return HTTP 404 with `{"success": false, "error": "<Entity> not found"}`
(`Server not found`, `Application not found`, `Service not found`).

The check is `cursor.rowcount == 0` after executing the statement, applied
identically in all six handlers (app.py:482-483, 502-503, 567-568,
587-588, 650-651, 670-671). Six consistent copies read as a decided
pattern, not an accident.

Confidence: high.
